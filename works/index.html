Works

Designing with intention

And love.

Working as a freelance graphic designer, I’ve developed a versatile practice across branding, visual identities, communication campaigns, editorial design and illustration. Working across different projects and contexts has taught me to adapt my creative approach while keeping a strong focus on concept, meaning and visual consistency. I’m particularly drawn to branding and visual communication. I believe every visual choice should have a purpose — from the overall concept and visual language to the smallest details. For me, a strong identity is one that feels coherent, distinctive and true to the project it represents.

Illustration is also an important part of my practice. I like bringing hand-drawn elements into my designs to add a more personal and expressive dimension, while creating visual worlds that feel unique and memorable.

Branding. Visual communication. Illustration. Graphic composition.
